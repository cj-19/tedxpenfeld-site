About

An evening of ideas in Brest, France, independently organised under license from TED.

Why come

Meet

Worlds that never cross paths, in the same room.

Discover

Ideas from well beyond your own field.

Hear new voices

Not professional speakers: people with something to say.

Keep watching

Every talk is filmed and published free on the TEDx channel.

The name

The Penfeld river cuts Brest in two, between Recouvrance and the city centre. A river that divides, and that you cross: that is exactly what we want to do.

The team

An entirely volunteer team, within Penfeld Conférences, a French non-profit association that has been preparing this evening for months.
