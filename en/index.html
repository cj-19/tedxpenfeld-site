TEDxPenfeld

A day of ideas in Brest, France. March 2027.

A researcher, a sailor, an artist, an engineer, a nurse. Ten minutes each, on the same stage, in front of the same audience — and no one came for any one of them in particular.

That format did not exist in Brest. We are building it.

One hundred seats. One day. Talks published free, for everyone, everywhere.

Why here

Brest has no shortage of academic conferences, festivals or industry events. What was missing was a general, demanding, public stage where worlds that never meet speak on the same day, to the same people.

An academic conference gathers peers. An industry event gathers a sector. A festival gathers around a practice. None of them manufactures the unlikely encounter.
