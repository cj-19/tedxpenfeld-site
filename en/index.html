One seat per world. One evening to cross.

Brest has no shortage of events. What it lacks is a place where they meet.

One evening, eight to nine talks, and one seat per world: the sea, digital, health, industry, culture. The Penfeld river divides the city; TEDxPenfeld helps people cross it.
