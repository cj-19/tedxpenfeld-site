TEDxPenfeld

TEDxPenfeld is an independently organised TEDx event, operated under license from TED, in Brest, France. The first edition takes place in March 2027.

The name comes from the Penfeld, the river that runs through Brest and along which the naval arsenal was built. The city was shaped by this river, and for a long time it remained a place its own inhabitants could not see.

The team

The event is run by Penfeld Conférences, an independent French non-profit association whose purpose is to organise conferences in Brest. We are members of it, and TEDxPenfeld is its first event.

The team is entirely volunteer, organised in seven areas: coordination, partnerships, curation, content, technical production, communications and logistics. No one is paid.
